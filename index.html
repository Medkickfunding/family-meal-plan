🍗 Sunday — Batch 1: Sheet-Pan Chicken

Halal Chicken Thighs + Broccoli + Sweet PotatoDinner • 6:30 PM
🍗
Sheet-pan dinner
Ingredients (serves 4):
  • 2 lbs halal chicken thighs, bone-in
  • 1 large head broccoli, cut into florets
  • 3 medium sweet potatoes, cubed
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, 4 garlic cloves minced
  • 1 tsp paprika, 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp salt, black pepper
  • 1 lemon, halved
How to cook: Preheat oven to 425°F. Toss sweet potatoes with 1 tbsp oil and salt on a sheet pan, roast 15 min. Add broccoli and chicken (rubbed with remaining oil, garlic, spices), roast 25–30 min more until chicken hits 165°F. Squeeze lemon over everything. Serve with 1½ cups cooked brown rice per person.

🍲 Wednesday — Batch 2: Lentil & Vegetable Soup

Lentil Soup with Whole Wheat PitaDinner • 6:30 PM
🍲
One-pot soup
Ingredients (serves 4):
  • 1½ cups dried brown lentils, rinsed
  • 1 onion diced, 2 carrots sliced, 2 celery stalks sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves minced, 1 can diced tomatoes
  • 6 cups low-sodium broth or water
  • 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp turmeric, salt & pepper
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, 4 whole wheat pitas
How to cook: Sauté onion, carrots, celery in olive oil 5 min. Add garlic, cumin, turmeric; cook 1 min. Add lentils, tomatoes, broth. Simmer 30–35 min until lentils are soft. Season. Serve with warm pita. Leftovers = Thursday lunch.

🍝 Friday — Batch 3: Turkey Meatballs & Pasta

Turkey Meatballs in Marinara over Whole Wheat PastaDinner • 6:30 PM
🍝
Kid-friendly favorite
Ingredients (serves 4):
  • 1½ lbs ground turkey (halal)
  • ½ cup breadcrumbs, 1 egg, ¼ cup grated parmesan
  • 2 garlic cloves minced, 1 tsp Italian seasoning, salt & pepper
  • 1 jar (24 oz) marinara sauce
  • 12 oz whole wheat pasta, side salad greens
How to cook: Mix turkey, breadcrumbs, egg, parmesan, garlic, seasoning. Roll into 1½-inch balls. Bake at 400°F for 18–20 min. Simmer meatballs in marinara 10 min. Boil pasta per package. Serve meatballs and sauce over pasta with a side salad. Leftovers cover Thursday dinner and Friday dinner.

🍳 No-Cook Meals

Breakfasts
🥣
Overnight oats
Overnight oats (Mon, Thu, Sat): ½ cup rolled oats + ½ cup milk + ¼ cup Greek yogurt + ½ cup berries + 1 tsp honey. Mix in a jar, refrigerate overnight.

Greek yogurt bowl (Sun, Wed): 1 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt + ½ cup berries + 2 tbsp oats + drizzle of honey.

Scrambled eggs (Tue, Fri): 2 eggs scrambled in 1 tsp oil, 1 slice whole wheat toast, ½ avocado.
Lunches
🌯
Wraps & hummus
Tuna wrap (Sun, Fri): 1 can tuna mixed with 2 tbsp Greek yogurt + lemon juice + celery, rolled in a whole wheat tortilla with lettuce.

Turkey wrap (Wed): 3 slices turkey, lettuce, tomato, 1 tbsp hummus in a tortilla.

Hummus plate (Mon, Sat): ⅓ cup hummus + 1 pita + cucumber slices + carrot sticks.

Cottage cheese (Tue): 1 cup low-fat cottage cheese + ½ cup berries + 1 tbsp oats.
